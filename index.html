Where ICE arrests the people held in Nebraska's immigration jails

Seven county jails and hold rooms in Nebraska take people into federal immigration detention. This page traces the route — where Immigration and Customs Enforcement recorded the arrest, the Nebraska facility that held the person, the out-of-state facility they were transferred to, and the country they were removed to — for every detention stay since late 2022 that touched one of the seven facilities.

Each record is one continuous stay in detention, matched to the arrest that began it. A stay can pass through more than one jail; it is counted here if any part of it was in a Nebraska facility. The arrest is often recorded at a local jail or state prison, not on the street — see how the arrests happen.

About the data

Source: the Deportation Data Project's processed “Arrests with detentions” dataset (ICE records obtained under FOIA; release of 21 August 2026). Filtered to detention stays whose facility list includes any of the seven Nebraska codes , then aggregated. No individual records are published here.

  • Arrest location is free text typed by ICE staff, with inconsistent spelling. City names were normalized and geocoded to a town centroid;
  • An arrest is where ICE took custody, not where the person lives. Most here are custodial — recorded at the jail or state prison already holding the person.
  • The Nebraska node is the first of the seven facilities a stay reaches: the Omaha ERO hold room unless a county-jail bed comes first in the record. Jail-to-jail hops inside the state are not drawn.
  • Transfers and removals use the last facility recorded for the stay. A stay is included if any stint touched a Nebraska facility, so some arrests and exit facilities sit in neighbouring states. Route legs with fewer than three stays are omitted.
  • Counts are detention stays (), not unique people — a few people appear twice after a release and re-arrest.
  • Rebuild: re-pull the filtered export from the Deportation Data Project explorer and run python3 build.py <file>.xlsx.
